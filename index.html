Who we are

Bringing Bangladesh to the heart of UML.

The Bangladesh Students Association is a student-led organization serving Bangladeshi students and anyone interested in Bangladeshi culture at the University of Massachusetts Lowell.

We create opportunities for students to connect, celebrate our heritage, support one another, and build a welcoming community throughout their university journey.
