# UMLপ্রাঙ্গণ

**UMLপ্রাঙ্গণ** is the official website of the **Bangladeshi Students' Association (BSA) at the University of Massachusetts Lowell**. It serves as a central platform for the Bangladeshi student community at UMass Lowell, bringing together association updates, events, community information, resources, and useful information for current and prospective students.

The website is maintained by BSA UML to preserve the association's activities, strengthen community connections, and provide an accessible home for information about the Bangladeshi community at UMass Lowell.
